# SeedStack

> **Sovereign legibility** is this repository's working name for its core discipline: AI workflows that stay local-first, human-readable, and evidence-backed. Related work studied *after* this architecture settled: Van Clief & McDermott's ICM (March 2026) — convergent design, independent lineage, different grounding.
>
> Repository note: Sovereign Stack is the public home of the SeedStack methodology project. First public commit: October 2026.

*Everyone else is teaching you to automate yourself. We're teaching you to stay in the loop.*

**SeedStack is an unofficial school for sovereign AI use** — a methodology project building the disciplines individuals and institutions need to use AI without surrendering judgment, data, or authority. Students certify by teaching. Frameworks are designed to be licensable to institutions. The community holds veto power over governance.

**Status:** early-stage, actively building. What is published here is real and usable; what is not yet published is still being exercised and logged. We would rather ship one proven protocol than forty promising drafts.

## Why this exists

AI tools make it trivially easy to produce fluent, plausible, *wrong* work. The failure mode is not dramatic — it is a confident claim nobody checked, shipping under someone's name. In governance, risk, and compliance work, where credibility is the product, that failure is existential.

The answer is not to avoid AI assistance. It is to build the verification habit until it is an auditable discipline rather than a personal virtue.

## Governance

SeedStack is built on the premise that governance without authority is decoration. The project is being built so the community holds real veto power over what ships. Details of the governance structure are published as they are exercised, not before.
